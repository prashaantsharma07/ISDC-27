Settings

Train your model

  1. Put one item in view, pick it below and press Burst (20 photos). Vary height, angle, light and background.
  2. Do this for every item (60–150 photos each).
  3. Run tools/autolabel.py then tools/train.py; restart the app and it uses your model.
⚠ No video signal. The capture adapter is connected but receives no picture from the drone receiver — check its power, channel and cable.
