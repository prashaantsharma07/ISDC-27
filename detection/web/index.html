Train your model

  1. Put one item in view, pick it below and press Burst (20 photos). Move: different heights, angles, light, backgrounds — like the competition field.
  2. Do this for every item (aim for 60–150 photos each).
  3. On this PC run tools/autolabel.py then tools/train.py — the app switches to your model on restart.
